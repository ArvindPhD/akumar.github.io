About Me

Passionate researcher dedicated to advancing fluid mechanics and computational modeling

Bio-Fluid Mechanics Researcher & CFD Specialist

I am a PhD scholar specializing in computational fluid dynamics with a strong focus on bio-fluid mechanics and thermal analysis. My academic journey spans from mechanical engineering fundamentals at A.U Tamil Nadu (B.Tech) to specialized thermal engineering at NIT Rourkela (M.Tech), and now pursuing doctoral research in advanced fluid mechanics and computational modeling.

With a solid foundation in thermal sciences and expertise in numerical modeling, I investigate complex flow phenomena ranging from microscale biological transport to large-scale industrial systems. My research employs state-of-the-art computational tools and mathematical frameworks to solve challenging problems in fluid dynamics, heat transfer, and environmental engineering.

Currently, my doctoral research explores the fascinating world of active particle dynamics, brake-generated particulate emissions, and their environmental impacts. My interdisciplinary approach combines mechanical engineering principles, thermal sciences, physics, and computational mathematics to address real-world challenges in sustainability and public health.
